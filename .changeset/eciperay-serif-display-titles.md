Eciperay page and recipe titles now render in the design brief's serif display type. Themes gain a `display` typography role, resolved through a registered Tamagui `display` font: the base role mirrors the UI sans stack, and the Warm Harvest (Eciperay) theme overrides it to the existing Georgia serif stack, so the title-bearing Text variants (`heading`, `title`, `formTitle`, `subheading`, `display`) — and Tamagui's `heading` font — render serif under Eciperay and keep the UI sans under every other theme (Memorize titles are unchanged and do not take the verse role's Alegreya). The serif titles render at reduced weight: themes carry `displayFontWeight` typography tokens (base: the standard semibold 600 / bold 700 steps), the registered `display` font's weight scale takes its semibold/bold slots from them, and Warm Harvest sets both title steps to medium (500) — Georgia ships only regular and bold faces and the semibold step renders identically to bold, so medium is the first step that visibly lightens the titles. Verify after adopting: under the Eciperay theme, page titles on the recipes, recipe detail, landing, home, meal-plan, profile, and recipe-form pages render in Georgia serif at medium weight (the regular serif face) while body text stays Raleway at its standard weights; under the Memorize theme, all titles still render in the UI sans at the standard title weights.
