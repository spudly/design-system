Form pages de-styled (#119, forms tranche): RecipeFormPage, MealFormPage, and MealPlanFormPage no longer apply any styling themselves, and this tranche adds no components — the pages compose only the shared form components (`FieldLabel`, `LabeledField`, `LabeledGroup`, `RevertAction`, `ChangedListBanner`, `FormStack`, `FormHeading` — shipped with the #119 dedupe set — plus `FieldColumns` and `FormSubmit` from the #119 extraction set). Behavior is unchanged: same fields, labels, steppers, banners, validation, and submission. The layouts are the shared components' own compositions: RecipeFormPage's status picker renders as toggle Buttons and its tags picker as selectable Badges, both in the shared ButtonRow; its servings / prep / cook fields render as one equal-width `FieldColumns` row, and its field labels (Title, Description, Servings, Prep, Cook, Source URL) are wired to their inputs, so on web clicking a label focuses its field; its scroll region is the shared `ScrollRegion` component (#301) — on web the region is keyboard-focusable, as the shared component specifies, and native rendering is unchanged. The meal forms' select lists render under a LabeledGroup label (the group carries the label as its name), the meal plan's per-entry steppers are grouped in FieldStack, and the meal forms' submit buttons sit in a `FormSubmit` row (32px below the last field: the layout's 16px gap plus the row's own 16px). On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/RecipeFormPage (Default: title, status picker, description, the servings/prep/cook fields, image, ingredients and instructions slots, tags, and the Create Recipe button stretched full width; ContentLocked: the lock notice in place of the ingredients and instructions slots, the other fields still editable; VersionDiff: the two changed-list banners with their Revert actions and the per-field revert actions beside the changed labels), Pages/Eciperay/MealFormPage (Default: title heading, fields, recipe list, per-recipe servings steppers, Save meal; NoSelection: Save meal disabled; SaveError: the error line above the submit row), and Pages/Eciperay/MealPlanFormPage (Default: title heading, date fields, entry list, quantity and servings steppers grouped per selected entry; NoSelection: Save plan disabled).
