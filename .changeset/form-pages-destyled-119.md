Form pages de-styled (#119, forms tranche): RecipeFormPage, MealFormPage, and MealPlanFormPage no longer apply any styling themselves — rendering, validation, and submission behavior are unchanged: same fields, labels, steppers, banners, and spacing. The pages now compose the shared form components (`FieldLabel`, `LabeledField`, `LabeledGroup`, `RevertAction`, `ChangedListBanner`, `FormStack`, `FormHeading` — shipped with the #119 dedupe set) plus four single-family components this tranche adds, all app-agnostic (content is caller-supplied): `FieldLabelBlock` (a standalone label block with its own 8px bottom padding, for a control that stands as its own form block below the label — the meal forms' recipe and entry checkbox lists), `FieldColumns` (a row of form fields in equal-width columns — the servings / prep / cook row), `FormSubmit` (a form's submit row, set off 16px below the last field), and `StepperGroup` (one entry's steppers kept flush together, with no gap of its own — the parent's rhythm separates one entry's group from the next). RecipeFormPage's status picker renders as toggle Buttons and its tags picker as selectable Badges, both in the shared ButtonRow; its scroll region is the shared `ScrollRegion` component (#301) — on web the region is keyboard-focusable, as the shared component specifies, and native rendering is unchanged. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/RecipeFormPage (Default: title, status picker, description, the servings/prep/cook columns, image, ingredients and instructions slots, tags, and the Save Changes button stretched full width; ContentLocked: the lock notice in place of the ingredients and instructions slots, the other fields still editable; VersionDiff: the two changed-list banners with their Revert actions and the per-field revert actions beside the changed labels), Pages/Eciperay/MealFormPage (Default: title heading, fields, recipe list, per-recipe servings steppers, Save meal; NoSelection: Save meal disabled; SaveError: the error line above the submit row), and Pages/Eciperay/MealPlanFormPage (Default: title heading, date fields, entry list, quantity and servings steppers grouped per selected entry; NoSelection: Save plan disabled). Also check the new components' stories against docs/manual-tests.md — Components/FieldLabelBlock, Components/FieldColumns, Components/FormSubmit, and Components/StepperGroup.
