Form pages de-styled (#119, forms tranche): RecipeFormPage, MealFormPage, and MealPlanFormPage no longer apply any styling themselves — rendering, validation, and submission behavior are unchanged: same fields, labels, steppers, banners, and spacing. Their pieces are now shared components, all app-agnostic (content is caller-supplied): `FieldLabel` (a form field's label text), `LabeledField` (a field with its label row above it — the label sits 4px over the field, an optional caller-supplied action such as a revert sits at the row's right edge, and an optional error renders below the field), `LabeledGroup` (the same label row for a control that is not a single field, such as a chip group or checkbox list, with the wider 8px label separation those controls carry), `RevertAction` (the "Revert to: <value>" warning action a form shows beside a changed field's label), `ChangedListBanner` (the warning banner announcing that a form's list section differs from its source, with an underlined Revert action when the caller offers one), `FieldColumns` (a row of form fields in equal-width columns — the servings / prep / cook row), `FormStack` (a form's fields and actions at the 16px form rhythm, children stretched to the column width, with 32px of tail space below the last field), `FormHeading` (a form page's title with its 24px separation from the first field), `FormSubmit` (a form's submit row, set off 16px below the last field), and `StepperGroup` (one entry's steppers kept flush together, with no gap of its own — the parent's rhythm separates one entry's group from the next). RecipeFormPage's scroll region is now the shared `ScrollRegion` component (#301); on web the region is keyboard-focusable, as the shared component specifies, and native rendering is unchanged. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/RecipeFormPage (Default: title, status chips, description, the servings/prep/cook columns, image, ingredients and instructions slots, tags, and the Save Changes button stretched full width; ContentLocked: the lock notice above the slots with the fields disabled; VersionDiff: the two changed-list banners with their Revert actions and the per-field revert actions beside the changed labels), Pages/Eciperay/MealFormPage (Default: title heading, fields, recipe list, per-recipe servings steppers, Save meal; NoSelection: Save meal disabled; SaveError: the error line above the submit row), and Pages/Eciperay/MealPlanFormPage (Default: title heading, date fields, entry list, quantity and servings steppers grouped per selected entry; NoSelection: Save plan disabled). Also check the new components' stories against docs/manual-tests.md — Components/FieldLabel, Components/LabeledField (Default, WithAction, WithError), Components/LabeledGroup (Default, WithAction), Components/RevertAction, Components/ChangedListBanner (Default, WithoutRevert), Components/FieldColumns, Components/FormStack, Components/FormHeading, Components/FormSubmit, and Components/StepperGroup.
