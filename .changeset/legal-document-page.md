New LegalDocumentPage in `@spudly/ui/pages` (design-system #170): one parameterized page renders both a privacy policy and a terms of service from structured content the host passes in — a title shown as the page's level-1 heading, a "Last updated: …" line under it, and sections with their own headings whose paragraphs mix plain, bold, and monospace spans and whose bullet lists lead items with a bold phrase. A link span in the content renders as a real link carrying the href from the data (for example a `mailto:` contact link mid-sentence). The page renders inside the full public frame: a header whose brand wordmark links home and whose sign-in link goes to the host's sign-in route, and a footer carrying the host's Privacy Policy / Terms of Service links. The document itself sits in a centered prose column capped at 768px (the public surfaces' column), so at a desktop-width viewport the title and sections stay in that column instead of stretching edge-to-edge, and bullet lists carry list/list-item semantics for screen readers on web. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check Pages/Eciperay/LegalDocumentPage in Storybook: the PrivacyPolicy story shows the three fixture sections with the bullet list under "Information we collect", the `noindex` code span under "Search engines", and the mailto link in "Contact us"; the TermsOfService story shows the same frame and layout driven by the terms fixture instead — different title, date, and sections, with nothing else changed.
