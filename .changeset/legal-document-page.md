New LegalDocumentPage in `@spudly/ui/pages` (design-system #170): one parameterized page renders both a privacy policy and a terms of service from a markdown string the host passes in, alongside a title shown as the page's level-1 heading and a "Last updated: …" line under it. In the markdown, `##` headings open sections rendered as level-2 headings, blank lines separate paragraphs, `-` lines form bullet lists, and `**bold**`, `` `code` ``, and `[label](href)` spans render inline — a markdown link renders as a real link carrying its href (for example a `mailto:` contact link mid-sentence). The page renders inside the full public frame: a header whose brand wordmark links home and whose sign-in link goes to the host's sign-in route, and a footer carrying the host's Privacy Policy / Terms of Service links. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check Pages/Eciperay/LegalDocumentPage in Storybook: the PrivacyPolicy story shows the three fixture sections with the bullet list under "Information we collect", the `noindex` code span under "Search engines", and the mailto link in "Contact us"; the TermsOfService story shows the same frame and layout driven by the terms markdown instead — different title, date, and sections, with nothing else changed.
