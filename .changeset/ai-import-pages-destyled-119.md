The AI session and recipe-import pages are de-styled (#119): AiSessionPage, ImportReviewPage, ImportImagePage, ImportTextPage, ImportUrlPage, and AddRecipeMenuPage now compose shared components with no page-level styling. Rendering is unchanged apart from the notes below. New shared components: `SessionShell` (the session frame — recessed page background, content padding, section rhythm, optional scrolling), `SessionHeader` (a session's form title plus an optional muted status line), `MessageList` (a two-role transcript: the user's messages indented from the right on a gray bubble, the assistant's from the left on a tinted bubble, each under its author label), `PreviewCard` (a bordered surface card with an optional title and an optional action row), and `CancelSessionAction` (the muted underlined "Cancel session" text action under a session's content). Button gains a `grow` prop (the button-form button stretches to fill its row's free space), and TextAreaField gains a `size` prop with semantic height steps (`md` the standard field height, `lg` a follow-up field, `xl` a full prompt field). Page notes: AiSessionPage's start action grows through `grow`, its prompt and follow-up fields take the `xl`/`lg` steps, its transcript renders through MessageList, its preview stage through PreviewCard, and its Cancel control is CancelSessionAction — same look and disabled behavior as before, and its keyboard activation now also refuses held-key repeats. ImportReviewPage's hero image renders through Thumbnail's `banner` presentation (same cover shape; a supplied image alt text names the image, an unnamed image stays decorative, as before) and its parsed preview sits in a PreviewCard under the form title. AddRecipeMenuPage's title takes Text's `title` variant — the same xl bold rendering and level-1 heading it had. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/AiSessionPage's Start, Planning, and PreviewReady stories (prompt field tall, Start button filling the row beside Cancel; transcript bubbles aligned by role with the Plan/You labels; preview card with its action; Cancel session muted and underlined at the bottom), Pages/Eciperay/ImportReviewPage (hero banner above the preview card; Looks good and Make edits buttons), the three import form pages (title, hint, and field rhythm unchanged), Pages/Eciperay/AddRecipeMenuPage (title and option cards unchanged), and the new component stories — Components/SessionShell (Fixed and Scrollable), Components/SessionHeader, Components/MessageList, Components/PreviewCard, Components/CancelSessionAction (Default and Disabled).
