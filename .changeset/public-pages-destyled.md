Public pages de-styled (#119): PublicPageFrame, DiscoverPage, LegalDocumentPage, and LandingPage now compose shared components and pass data and callbacks only — their rendering is unchanged. The frame renders through the new PublicFrameLayout, PublicFrameHeader, PublicFrameFooter, and BrandLink components — BrandLink is a real anchor: the frame takes a brandHref (the host's home route) instead of a brand press callback, and the header wordmark renders as a link carrying that href; the legal document renders through DocumentHeader, DocumentFooter, and the new ProseDocument structured-prose renderer (the page's Legal\* content types now alias its Prose\* model); the landing renders through the new Hero, CtaRow, FeatureCard, and CardGrid components, its CTAs rendered as Button href links (ButtonLink — the duplicate CtaLink component is removed); and DiscoverPage's catalog renders through the shared BoundedList with the new CatalogHeader and CatalogFiltersBar components and the shared ListLoadingState, ListErrorState, and ListLoadingFooter list states. CatalogFiltersBar is definition-driven: which filters exist, their labels, option sets, and defaults are the caller's data passed in as filter definitions — the bar owns the control shapes only. The list states carry no offset of their own: the loading states center their spinner with Center, and the parent composition owns the spacing around every state.
