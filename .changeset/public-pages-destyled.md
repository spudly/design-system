Public pages de-styled (#119): PublicPageFrame, DiscoverPage, LegalDocumentPage, and LandingPage now compose shared components and pass data only — their rendering is unchanged. The frame renders through the shared PublicFrameLayout, PublicFrameHeader, PublicFrameFooter, and BrandLink components, and its navigation is hrefs throughout: the frame takes brandHref (the host's home route), signInHref, privacyHref, and termsHref instead of press callbacks — the wordmark renders as a real anchor carrying brandHref, Sign in as a link carrying signInHref, and the footer receives its Privacy/Terms links as {label, href} data built from the frame's labels and the host's hrefs. The legal document renders through DocumentHeader, DocumentFooter, and the ProseDocument structured-prose renderer (the page's Legal* content types alias its Prose* model); the landing renders through the new Hero, CtaRow, FeatureCard, and CardGrid components, its CTAs rendered as Button href links (the duplicate CtaLink component is removed); and DiscoverPage's catalog renders through the shared BoundedList with the shared CatalogHeader and CatalogFiltersBar components and the shared ListLoadingState — its error state renders an Alert directly and its loading-more footer renders ListLoadingState (there are no separate list error/footer state components). CatalogFiltersBar is definition-driven: which filters exist, their labels, option sets, and defaults are the caller's data passed in as filter definitions — the bar owns the control shapes only. The list states carry no offset of their own: the loading state centers its spinner with Center, and the parent composition owns the spacing around every state.
