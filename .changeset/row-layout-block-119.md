New `Row` layout component and the first #119 page-styling fixes. `Row` (exported from `@spudly/ui`) lays children out horizontally with token-driven gaps chosen through semantic props — `spacing` (`none`/`sm`/`md`), `align` (`start`/`center`/`end`/`stretch`), and `spaceBefore` (`none`/`sm`/`md`) — so pages compose rows without applying styling props themselves. `SearchField` gains a `grow` prop: in a row, the field takes the remaining width while sibling controls keep their natural width. VerseSearchPage, MemorizeReviewPage, and MyVersesPage are rebuilt on these blocks (their lists now render through a shared bounded list that owns the list bounding and the 8px row separation): rendering is unchanged — same headings, cards, buttons, and spacing. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Components/Row's Actions story shows two small buttons side by side with an even gap, Centered shows the search field filling the row beside the Search button with both vertically centered, and SpaceBefore shows the action row set off below the verse text; Pages/Memorize/VerseSearchPage (Default, WithAddedResult), Pages/Memorize/MemorizeReviewPage (Default), and Pages/Memorize/MyVersesPage (Default) render as before, with 8px between result cards and the lists scrolling inside the fixed shell.
