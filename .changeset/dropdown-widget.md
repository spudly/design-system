New Dropdown widgets (adopted from shuoink/ui, design-system #178): `Dropdown` is an anchored dropdown shell — it positions a panel against its trigger (below by default, or above; aligned to the trigger's start or end edge, flipping/shifting at viewport edges), tracks open state (controlled via `open`/`onOpenChange` or self-managed from `defaultOpen`), and dismisses on outside press or Escape. It has a single slot for content the caller composes from DS building blocks — typically action rows that close the menu when chosen, via the exported `useDropdownClose` hook; there is no built-in item list. `DropdownButton` is the trigger the DS renders for it: a Button-described-by-props (label and/or a DS icon, variant, size) rendering the label, an optional leading icon, and a disclosure chevron that flips while the menu is open, with `aria-haspopup` and the expanded state wired for assistive tech; with no label it is an icon-only trigger and requires an `accessibilityLabel`. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Components/Dropdown's ActionMenu story opens the panel under the "Recipe actions" button, choosing "Print recipe" closes the menu and updates the "Last action" line, and InitiallyOpen renders the panel already open; Components/DropdownButton's IconOnly story shows the profile glyph + chevron trigger named "Account menu", and its Variants story opens a menu from each of the primary, secondary, and ghost triggers.
