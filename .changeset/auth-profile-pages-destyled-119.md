The auth, profile, feedback, and memorize-settings pages are rebuilt on the shared component library (#119): SignInPage, SignUpPage, ForgotPasswordPage, ResetPasswordPage, ProfilePage, FeedbackPage, and MemorizeSettingsPage now compose design-system components only, with the layout owned by the components instead of styling set in the pages. The four auth forms ship as standalone components — `SignInForm`, `SignUpForm`, `ForgotPasswordForm`, `ResetPasswordForm` — each rendering its fields, error line, and submit button with the same Enter-to-submit behavior and loading states the pages had, plus `LegalSection`, the titled legal-links block ProfilePage frames its host links with. Text gains two variants: `muted` (body-size text in the muted treatment — the intro line under the auth pages' titles and the Profile page's email line) and `success` (small text in the success treatment — the Feedback page's "Thanks — we got your feedback." line). TextLink gains a `tone="muted"` treatment for de-emphasized destinations ("Forgot password?", "Back to sign in"), matching the app link button's muted variant. Spacing follows the app screens the design system mirrors: on the auth pages the title-to-form and form-internal separations now match the app views exactly (SignInPage's footer links stack tighter, at 8px); on ProfilePage the blocks separate at the shell's 16px rhythm (title to email reads tighter than before); on FeedbackPage the fields separate at the form's 16px rhythm throughout and the message field's label matches the email field's label treatment; on MemorizeSettingsPage the two button pairs sit 12px apart (ButtonRow) instead of 8px. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: the four auth pages (Pages/Eciperay) submit on Enter from every field and show their footer links in the treatments above; ProfilePage (Pages/Eciperay) shows its sections, Legal block, and Sign Out in order; FeedbackPage (Pages/Eciperay) submits to the success thanks line; MemorizeSettingsPage (Pages/Memorize) keeps its API-key and Drive sections working; and the new components render as documented in Components/SignInForm, Components/SignUpForm, Components/ForgotPasswordForm, Components/ResetPasswordForm, and Components/LegalSection.
