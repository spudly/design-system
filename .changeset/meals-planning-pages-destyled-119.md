Meals & planning pages de-styled (#119, meals & planning tranche): MealDetailPage, MealsHubPage, ShoppingListPage, TagsPage, HomePage, and MealCookingModePage no longer apply any styling themselves — they compose shared components and pass data, callbacks, and hrefs only, and their titles take Text's `title` variant (HomePage is the pinned exception: its hero title keeps the `heading` variant and its section title the `subheading` variant). Rendering is faithful, with the spacing normalizations listed below. The pieces they needed are now shared components, all app-agnostic (content is caller-supplied): `CheckRow` (a check-off row whose whole body is the checkbox — a drawn box and a bold label with muted detail segments inline over a hairline; ShoppingListPage's lines, the page composing each line's quantity/optional detail), `TextActionCardRow` (an elevated card row whose text block is a link and whose trailing action is a button-role text action — MealsHubPage's meal rows, whose Delete stays a TextAction per the #248 ruling), `BadgeCardRow` (a compact card row presenting a colored Badge with trailing text actions — TagsPage's tag rows: the badge's label and tag color name cross as data, since a page may not set Badge's color itself), `StepOverlay` (a full-screen step-reader shell — dark absolute-fill scrim, translucent Done pill, eyebrow lines over the step body at reading size — with the caller's step navigation composed below; MealCookingModePage's cooking steps), and `PinnedHeaderShell` (a page shell holding a header pinned above a self-scrolling content region — MealsHubPage's plans tab over MealPlansPage). Layout and content blocks, each named for what it contains and owning its spacing: `FieldStack` (one form's fields at the 12px field rhythm), `TrailingActionsRow` (a leading group and its actions spread to opposite ends, wrapping when they cannot fit — the #219 row TagsPage's forms and tag rows share), `CompactCard` (the small surface card — 12px padding, medium radius, small shadow — for dense rows and inline editors; TagsPage's edit card), `ButtonStack` (a stack of full-width buttons at 12px — ButtonRow's vertical form; HomePage's actions), and `CenteredText` (a Text line with centering baked in and an optional muted treatment; HomePage's subtitle and empty message). MealDetailPage's member rows render through ListRow's link form (available members link; an unavailable member takes the disabled state and the page-supplied " (unavailable)" suffix). Spacing normalizations to verify: MealsHubPage's meal cards sit at CardStack's 8px rhythm (each card previously carried a 12px bottom padding); MealDetailPage's header blocks separate at SectionStack's uniform 32px (notes, nutrition, and the Cook/Edit row sat 16–24px below their predecessors); TagsPage's form-to-list separation is 32px (was 40px) and its edit form's Cancel/Save gap is 12px (was 8px), and a tag row now wraps at narrow widths like the form rows do instead of squeezing; HomePage's subtitle-to-buttons separation is 32px (was 24px), its loading spinner and empty message no longer add their own 32px top offset, the screen's extra 8px top padding is gone, and its section title takes the `subheading` variant's semibold weight (was bold). ShoppingListPage and MealCookingModePage render exactly as before.
