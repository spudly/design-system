StepOverlay (#332): two behavior-contract fixes. First, the step body and the caller-composed navigation now sit in a bounded scroll region: a long step body on a short viewport scrolls instead of clipping at both ends of the centered column, the navigation stays reachable at the bottom of the region, short bodies keep the vertically centered treatment, and the Done pill stays pinned to the top right — fixed and reachable at any scroll position. Second, the shell manages focus: it carries dialog semantics (a modal dialog named for its title on web, view-modal containment on native) and moves keyboard focus into itself when it mounts, so a host that mounts it over still-mounted interactive content no longer leaves that content as the keyboard or screen-reader starting point — the host does not need to unmount or inert the covered content. Tab and Shift+Tab wrap at the overlay's edges instead of escaping to the covered content, and when the overlay closes, focus returns to the element that had it before the overlay opened. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Components/StepOverlay (Default: body vertically centered, Done pill focusable with Enter/Space; LongBody: scroll the region to reach the dots and the Back / Next row with the Done pill pinned, and on open focus lands on the overlay) and Pages/Eciperay/MealCookingModePage (LongBody: the long step scrolls in the short frame with the Done pill pinned at the top right), against docs/manual-tests.md.
