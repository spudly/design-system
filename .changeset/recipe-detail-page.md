Add `RecipeDetailPage` to the `@spudly/ui/pages` subpath (spudly/design-system#49): Eciperay's recipe detail screen as a controlled page component, following the RecipesPage pilot. The app keeps all state and business logic (queries, mutations, navigation, printing) and drives the view through DS-owned per-tab view-models — chrome (status badge, version picker, the five tabs), overview (hero image, stat band, action chips, lifecycle actions with activation candidates, tags, a ratings card with a controlled viewer star input), ingredients (servings stepper, scale chips, tappable scaled rows, substitutions panel), instructions (step cards with an active step), cook history (entries plus the "Log a cook" button), and nutrition (loading / unavailable / ready states). Three self-contained app panels enter through documented render slots at the positions they occupy today — `renderCookLogForm` (replaces the "Log a cook" button while it renders content), `renderSharingControls`, and `renderComments` — so their state and mutations stay app-side. Loading is a `{ state: "loading" }` props member rendering only the spinner shell, mirroring the app screen, which has no distinct not-found UI.
