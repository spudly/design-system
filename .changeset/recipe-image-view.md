RecipeFormPage's image section renders from props (#130, Workstream A). The page gains an `image` data prop carrying the image field's view-half contract approved on #124 — the current image's preview URI, the upload / AI-generation busy flags, a host-reported error, and the AI-generation availability gate — and renders the new internal RecipeImageView from it: the labeled preview frame (the current image, or the empty-state line), the Browse images / Take photo / Generate with AI / Remove image actions with their in-flight labels and disabled states, and the error alert. The page supplies the view's header action itself (the image revert affordance, so VersionDiff forms keep their revert in the section's label row). Picking, capture, cropping, upload, and the AI call stay with the host — the view reports bare intents (`onPickImageRequest`, `onTakePhotoRequest`, `onGenerateImageRequest`, `onRemoveImage`) and never calls a picker library. The `renderImageSection` slot remains as the fallback for hosts that have not adopted the data prop, and the sortable-list slots are unchanged.
