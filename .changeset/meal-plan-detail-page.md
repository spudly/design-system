Add MealPlanDetailPage (spudly/design-system#49), the meal plan detail screen as a controlled page component behind the `@spudly/ui/pages` subpath, following the RecipesPage pilot and MealPlansPage. The app keeps the plan query and navigation, and drives the view through a state union — `{ state: "loading" }` renders only the spinner shell (the app screen gates on loading and has no not-found UI, so the union has no such member) and `{ state: "ready", ... }` renders the detail: plan title, notes, and pre-composed `dateRangeLabel` / `nutritionLabel` strings, the "Nutrition goals" section (per-field goal rows as pre-composed `valueLabel`s, or the app's set-goals hint, with `onSetGoalsPress` for its link), the Shopping list and Edit actions, and the flat entry list. Entries are `{id, kind, targetId, title, subtitle?, isUnavailable?}` view-models: meal rows report `onMealPress(mealId)` and recipe rows `onRecipePress(recipeId)`, and an access-lost recipe row renders its "(unavailable)" suffix without press wiring. The goals section is view-modeled rather than slotted — the app's goal view is pure presentation over data — so the contract carries no render slot, and there is no delete or entry-editing surface, matching the app screen (those live on the plan list and form screens).
