New auth form components (#119), shipped standalone so every app composes the same form units: `SignInForm` (email and password fields, an error line above the submit button, and the create-account and forgot-password footer links — the forgot-password link in TextLink's muted tone, each link rendered only when the host passes its href), `SignUpForm` (display name, email, and password fields, the error line, the submit button, a caller-rendered legal-notice slot, and the sign-in link), `ForgotPasswordForm` (the email field, the error line, the submit button, and a muted back-to-sign-in link), and `ResetPasswordForm` (the new-password field, the error line, and the submit button). Each form renders its fields in the shared `FormStack` rhythm, submits on Enter from any of its fields, and holds submission while loading; credentials cross only as controlled values with change callbacks and submission as a bare intent — validation, the auth call, and error mapping stay with the host, whose error crosses back as a string, and the reset token never crosses the design-system boundary at all. Also new: `LegalSection`, a titled legal-links block — a level-2 section title in the theme's text ink over the host's legal links, which cross as children (the URLs stay app content). On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Components/SignInForm (Default: Enter in either field submits; WithError: the error line sits above the submit button; Loading: submission is held; WithoutLinks: no footer links render), Components/SignUpForm (Default: the legal notice renders between the submit button and the sign-in link), Components/ForgotPasswordForm and Components/ResetPasswordForm (Default: Enter submits; WithError and Loading as above), and Components/LegalSection (Default: the title over the caller's links), against docs/manual-tests.md.
