The utility page literals move to the app-supplied voice object, as tranche 3b of the voice-as-theme system (#539): FeedbackPage's Feedback title, Share ideas, confusion, or problems. hint, Thanks — we got your feedback. submitted message, What's on your mind? message label, Tell us what happened or what you'd like to see improved. message placeholder, Email me back (optional) reply-email label, you@example.com reply-email placeholder, This is a bug checkbox label, and Submit feedback / Submitting… submit labels; ConsentPreferencesPage's Privacy choices card title, analytics description and withdraw hint defaults, Your current choice: line with its Accepted / Declined / Not set choice labels, and Accept / Decline / Withdraw action labels; and RouteErrorPage's Page not found and Something went wrong title defaults, their message defaults, and Go to the home page home-link label default. Apps must supply the new feedbackPage, consentPreferencesPage, and routeErrorPage voice slots through VoiceProvider; Storybook's fixture voice carries the same strings these pages previously hard-coded, so rendered output is unchanged. AuthConfirmationPage is unchanged: its title, body, and back label were already caller-supplied props with no hard-coded copy.
