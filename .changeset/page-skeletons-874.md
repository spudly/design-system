Four more pages render skeleton loading states (#874, following the #278 pattern), so hosts can mount them before their data resolves instead of gating on a spinner. MealDetailPage gains an optional `loading` prop: while loading, a title bone and two meta-line bones stand in for the meal's title, notes, and nutrition line, the Cook meal / Edit row renders as inert skeleton buttons, and the Recipes section holds exactly three skeleton member rows (the direct-ingredients section is omitted while loading, since whether the meal has any is part of the pending data). MyVersesPage gains an optional `loading` prop: the "My verses" heading stays and the list region renders exactly three skeleton card rows, with neither the verses nor the empty state rendered. CookingModePage gains an optional `loading` prop, checked before its out-of-range rule so a host may hold no steps yet: the full-screen shell renders a title bone, a step-label bone, and four body-line bones. ConsentPreferencesPage gains an optional `loading` prop: its section card renders in its loading state with description bones, the Accept / Decline / Withdraw row as inert skeleton buttons, and a hint bone, and no recorded choice renders. Each page gains a `Loading` story pinning the state. Verify after adopting: passing `loading` to any of the four pages shows its skeleton with no real data content, and omitting it renders each page exactly as before. This is a partial tranche of #874 — the remaining pages and views without skeleton states are inventoried in the pull request's audit table and follow in a later tranche.
