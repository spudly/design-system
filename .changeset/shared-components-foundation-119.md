Shared-components foundation for the #119 page de-styling program (Stephen's sequencing, 2026-10-29: shared components first, then the remaining page tranches in parallel by family). No page changes in this release — pages adopt these pieces in their tranches; every existing component renders exactly as before. New components, all app-agnostic with caller-supplied content: `ActionChip` (the small tonal action chip pages used to hand-style out of Button — `tone`: `neutral` gray fill, `danger` soft danger, `success` soft success, `primary` primary-tinted outline, `outline` hairline; `size`: `sm`/`md`; an optional DS `icon` renders left of the label in the tone's ink, and icon-only chips are square 44/48px targets that require an `accessibilityLabel`), `Thumbnail` (a content image owning its presentation — `variant`: `hero` in its 256px recessed frame with the image contained, `banner` full-width 192px cover with large rounded corners, `row` the 64px cover-cropped list thumbnail on its gray well; an `accessibilityLabel` names the image when it carries meaning), `ScrollRegion` (the inner scroll region of a fixed-shell screen — fills the space the shell leaves it, taps keep landing with the keyboard open; for shells ScreenContainer does not scroll), and `StatBand` (the recessed gray band that spreads its caller-supplied StatCards evenly across the page width). `Text` gains two variants for the treatments pages hand-applied identically: `label` (the section eyebrow — xs semibold, uppercase, letter-spaced, muted gray; not a heading) and `formTitle` (the form-flow page title — 2xl bold, exposed as a level-1 heading). Internally, the pages' shared `BoundedList` scaffolding gains the catalog pattern as optional slots (header/empty/footer, end-reached pagination, scroll handler, keyboard tap persistence, the standard page content inset, and an `md`/`none` separator choice) — all defaults unchanged, so the lists that use it today render identically. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Components/ActionChip (Tones: five labeled chips — gray Edit, soft-red Delete, soft-green Mark perfected, primary-outlined Tweak with AI, hairline Add to plan; WithIcon: Print/Share chips with glyphs in the label ink; IconOnly: two square 48px glyph targets; Sizes: sm and md rows; Disabled: both chips dimmed and inert), Components/Thumbnail (Hero: image contained in the recessed frame; Banner: full-width cover image with rounded corners; Row: the 64px square), Components/ScrollRegion (Default: header and footer stay pinned while the middle scrolls), Components/StatBand (Default: Servings/Prep/Cook spread evenly on the gray band; TwoStats), and Components/Text (FormTitle: 2xl bold with heading level 1; Label: uppercase eyebrow, no heading role; AllVariants shows both in the set) against docs/manual-tests.md.
