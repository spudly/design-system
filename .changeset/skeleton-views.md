Views gain skeleton loading states (#278, tranche 3 of 5). AccessTokensPanel, AccountDataView, CandidateList, CatalogHeader, ChangedListBanner, FeedbackPreferenceView, LegalSection, NutritionGoalsForm, RecipeCommentsView, RecipeExportPickerView, RecipeImageView, RecipeSharingView, ScaledIngredientsView, SubscriptionView, and TransferRecipesView each accept a `loading` prop: while loading, their text runs swap for text skeletons at the same sizes and counts, images swap for box skeletons at the same frame, their actions stay in place as inert skeleton buttons, hairlines render transparent, and no real content, values, or live controls render; the loading region is marked busy for assistive tech. Views that render other views pass `loading` down (TransferRecipesView forwards it to the export picker). RecipeCommentsView and SubscriptionView no longer render a loading spinner — the comments list renders skeleton comment items and the subscription section renders skeleton plan/status lines — AccessTokensPanel's "Loading tokens…" line is replaced by skeleton token rows, and LazySlot's Suspense fallback renders a generic skeleton composition (box and text bones filling the slot) instead of a spinner. Test: render each named view with `loading` (or open its Loading story) and confirm skeletons appear with no real text, values, or actions; compare its LoadingOverlay story against the loaded layer and confirm the shapes align; render without `loading` and confirm the view is unchanged.
