Add MealPlansPage (spudly/design-system#49): Eciperay's meal plan list — the "Meal plans" tab of the app's Meals hub — as a controlled full-page component, exported from the `@spudly/ui/pages` subpath alongside RecipesPage. The app keeps all state and business logic and drives the page entirely through props: plan view-models ({id, title, subtitle}, with the meta line — entry count · date range · nutrition — pre-composed by the app) and press callbacks out (onPlanPress, plus optional onCreatePlan and onPlanDelete, whose actions render only when the callbacks are provided). The composition mirrors the app's hub rows: a scrolling shell, the create action above the list, one elevated card per plan, loading as a content swap (the app has no filter inputs whose focus a swap would drop), and the app's "No meal plans yet." empty state. It is deliberately not the week grid the #44 demo imagined — the real app has no day/week structure — and it carries no render slot: an audit of the app view found no app-rendered content inside the list rows (creation and entry editing live on the separate plan form screen), so every action is a pure intent callback; a future inline affordance would add a slot by the renderRecipeAccessory precedent. Stories render the real component with fixture props in the Warm Harvest theme.
