Meal-plan pages de-styled (#119, tranche 2) and the layout blocks they needed. MealPlansPage and MealPlanDetailPage no longer apply any styling themselves — rendering is unchanged: same headings, cards, rows, buttons, and spacing. Their pieces are now shared components, all app-agnostic (content is caller-supplied): `CardRow` (a titled card row for lists: title over an optional meta line as the press target, with an optional trailing action button — the plan list's Delete is its danger tone), `ListRow` (a hairline list row whose title line composes a caller-supplied prefix, the title, and a caller-supplied suffix over an optional meta line, with a generic disabled treatment — the detail page's entry rows, with the "Meal · " / "Recipe · " prefixes and " (unavailable)" suffix supplied by the page), `DetailRow` (a muted label and its value over a hairline — the nutrition-goal rows), and `LoadingGate` (the full-screen centered-spinner state a detail page shows while loading). New `Column` layout component: Row's vertical counterpart. Both take ONE semantic prop — a `variant` naming the role the container plays — and the role pins the gap, alignment, and wrapping inside the component, so spacing is identical wherever a role is used and call sites pass no spacing at all (no scale steps, no margin props; separation between blocks is the parent stack's role gap). Column's roles: `sections` (a page's major blocks at the 32px page rhythm), `group` (related content inside a card, 12px), `list` (rows of one list, 8px), `state` (a centered page-state block). Row's roles: `actions` (a button row following content — 12px gap, wraps at narrow widths, the standard 8px separation from the content it follows) and `inline` (controls composed on one line, vertically centered, 8px gap). Row's tranche-1 scale-step props are replaced by this role API, and its existing consumers are reworked onto it — three spacing normalizations to verify on a bump: the Memorize card-footer buttons (MemorizeReviewPage, MyVersesPage) now sit at the action bar's 12px gap (was 8px); MealPlanDetailPage's section separations are now the uniform 32px sections gap (actions→entries was 40px under the old per-block margins; goals→actions is 40px — the 32px gap plus the action bar's own standard 8px separation — was 48px; and the goals card's internal lead/follow separations were 24px, now the group's uniform 12px); MealPlansPage's loading spinner sits at the shell's 16px separation below the create button (was 48px). Everything else renders as before. Text gains a `title` variant — xl bold, exposed as a level-1 heading — for detail-screen titles like the plan title. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/MealPlansPage (Default shows the create button full width above the plan cards with each card's title over its meta line; Loading centers the spinner 16px below the button; NoActions drops the create and Delete buttons) and Pages/Eciperay/MealPlanDetailPage (Default: xl bold plan title, muted meta lines, Nutrition goals card with label/value rows, Shopping list / Edit side by side, kind-prefixed entry rows; Loading shows only the centered gate; NoGoals hides the goals card).
