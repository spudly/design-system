Meal-plan pages de-styled (#119, tranche 2) and the layout blocks they needed. MealPlansPage and MealPlanDetailPage no longer apply any styling themselves — rendering is unchanged: same headings, cards, rows, buttons, and spacing. Their pieces are now shared components: `MealPlanRow` (the plan list's elevated card row: title over its meta line as the press target, with the soft-danger Delete beside it when the delete action is wired), `MealPlanEntryRow` (a plan's entry row: the "Meal · " / "Recipe · " kind prefix over an optional meta line, hairline below; an unavailable recipe entry gains its " (unavailable)" suffix and does not press), `DetailRow` (a muted label and its value over a hairline — the nutrition-goal rows), and `LoadingGate` (the full-screen centered-spinner state a detail page shows while loading). New `Column` layout component: Row's vertical counterpart, stacking children with token-driven gaps. Row and Column share one spacing-step vocabulary — `spacing`, `spaceBefore`, and `spaceAfter` accept `none`/`sm`/`md`/`lg`/`xl`/`2xl` (0/8/12/16/24/32px) — and Row gains `spaceAfter` and a `wrap` flag (children flow onto the next line at narrow widths). Note: the step named `md` is now 12px (it was 16px, which is now `lg`); Row is unpublished and no page used `md`, so nothing rendered changes. Text gains a `title` variant — xl bold, exposed as a level-1 heading — for detail-screen titles like the plan title. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/MealPlansPage (Default shows the create button full width above the plan cards with each card's title over its meta line; Loading centers the spinner 32px below the button; NoActions drops the create and Delete buttons) and Pages/Eciperay/MealPlanDetailPage (Default: xl bold plan title, muted meta lines, Nutrition goals card with label/value rows, Shopping list / Edit side by side, kind-prefixed entry rows; Loading shows only the centered gate; NoGoals hides the goals card). Also check Components/Column (Stacked, Centered, SpaceAfter), Components/Row (the step controls move gaps and surrounding space by 0/8/12/16/24/32px), Components/Text's Title story (xl bold, heading level 1), and the new Components/MealPlanRow, Components/MealPlanEntryRow, Components/DetailRow, and Components/LoadingGate stories against docs/manual-tests.md.
