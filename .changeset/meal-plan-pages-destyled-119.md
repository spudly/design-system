Meal-plan pages de-styled (#119, tranche 2) and the layout blocks they needed. MealPlansPage and MealPlanDetailPage no longer apply any styling themselves — rendering is unchanged: same headings, cards, rows, buttons, and spacing. Their pieces are now shared components, all app-agnostic (content is caller-supplied): `CardRow` (a titled card row for lists: title over an optional meta line as the press target, with an optional trailing action button — the plan list's Delete is its danger tone), `ListRow` (a hairline list row whose title line composes a caller-supplied prefix, the title, and a caller-supplied suffix over an optional meta line, with a generic disabled treatment — the detail page's entry rows, with the "Meal · " / "Recipe · " prefixes and " (unavailable)" suffix supplied by the page), `DetailRow` (a muted label and its value over a hairline — the nutrition-goal rows), and `LoadingGate` (the full-screen centered-spinner state a detail page shows while loading). The layout blocks are purpose-named components — one per use case, named for what it contains, each owning its spacing outright; there is no generic parameterized stack in the page-facing API, and no spacing, role, or margin props anywhere (margins as props are abolished; separation between blocks is the parent stack's gap). New: `ButtonRow` (a row of action buttons following content — 12px gap, wraps at narrow widths, the standard 8px separation from the content it follows), `CardStack` (a stack of cards at the 8px list rhythm), `SectionStack` (a page's sections at the 32px page rhythm), `FieldRow` (a row of form fields and their action, centered, 8px gap), `ListStack` (hairline rows as one continuous list — no gap of its own; the rows own their separation), and `Center` (centers a single child; boolean `horizontal`/`vertical`, both default true). The tranche-1 `Row` (scale-step props) is replaced by these before ever shipping in a release, and its consumers are reworked onto them: VerseSearchPage's search row is a FieldRow, the Memorize card footers are ButtonRows, MealPlansPage's list is a CardStack and its loading block a Center, MealPlanDetailPage's blocks are children of one SectionStack with a ButtonRow action bar. `SectionCard` now owns its content spacing (a uniform 12px gap between its children below the header): the goals card's content rhythm comes from the card itself, and the other SectionCard consumers take the same card rhythm — check NutritionGoalsForm's fields and AccessTokensPanel's blocks on a bump. Spacing normalizations to verify: the Memorize card-footer buttons now sit at ButtonRow's 12px gap (was 8px); MealPlanDetailPage's section separations are the uniform 32px SectionStack gap (actions→entries was 40px under the old per-block margins; goals→actions is 40px — the 32px gap plus the bar's own standard 8px separation — was 48px; the goals card's internal lead/follow separations were 24px, now the card's uniform 12px); MealPlansPage's loading spinner sits at the shell's 16px separation below the create button (was 48px). Everything else renders as before. Text gains a `title` variant — xl bold, exposed as a level-1 heading — for detail-screen titles like the plan title. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/MealPlansPage (Default shows the create button full width above the plan cards with each card's title over its meta line; Loading centers the spinner 16px below the button; NoActions drops the create and Delete buttons) and Pages/Eciperay/MealPlanDetailPage (Default: xl bold plan title, muted meta lines, Nutrition goals card with label/value rows, Shopping list / Edit side by side, kind-prefixed entry rows; Loading shows only the centered gate; NoGoals hides the goals card). Also check the new layout components' stories — Components/ButtonRow (Default), Components/CardStack (Default), Components/SectionStack (Default), Components/FieldRow (Default), Components/ListStack (Default), Components/Center (Centered, HorizontalOnly) — Components/Text's Title story (xl bold, heading level 1), and the new Components/CardRow, Components/ListRow, Components/DetailRow, and Components/LoadingGate stories against docs/manual-tests.md.
