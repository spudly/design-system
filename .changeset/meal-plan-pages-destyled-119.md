Meal-plan pages de-styled (#119, tranche 2) and the layout blocks they needed. MealPlansPage and MealPlanDetailPage no longer apply any styling themselves — rendering is unchanged: same headings, cards, rows, buttons, and spacing. Their pieces are now shared components, all app-agnostic (content is caller-supplied): `CardRow` (a titled card row for lists: title over an optional meta line as the press target, with an optional trailing action button — the plan list's Delete is its danger tone), `ListRow` (a hairline list row whose title line composes a caller-supplied prefix, the title, and a caller-supplied suffix over an optional meta line, with a generic disabled treatment — the detail page's entry rows, with the "Meal · " / "Recipe · " prefixes and " (unavailable)" suffix supplied by the page), `DetailRow` (a muted label and its value over a hairline — the nutrition-goal rows), and `LoadingGate` (the full-screen centered-spinner state a detail page shows while loading). New `Column` layout component: Row's vertical counterpart, stacking children with token-driven gaps. Row and Column share one spacing-step vocabulary — `spacing`, `spaceBefore`, and `spaceAfter` accept `none`/`sm`/`md`/`lg`/`xl`/`2xl` (0/8/12/16/24/32px) — and Row gains `spaceAfter` and a `wrap` flag (children flow onto the next line at narrow widths). Note: the step named `md` is now 12px (it was 16px, which is now `lg`); Row is unpublished and no page used `md`, so nothing rendered changes. Text gains a `title` variant — xl bold, exposed as a level-1 heading — for detail-screen titles like the plan title. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/MealPlansPage (Default shows the create button full width above the plan cards with each card's title over its meta line; Loading centers the spinner 32px below the button; NoActions drops the create and Delete buttons) and Pages/Eciperay/MealPlanDetailPage (Default: xl bold plan title, muted meta lines, Nutrition goals card with label/value rows, Shopping list / Edit side by side, kind-prefixed entry rows; Loading shows only the centered gate; NoGoals hides the goals card). Also check Components/Column (Stacked, Centered, SpaceAfter), Components/Row (the step controls move gaps and surrounding space by 0/8/12/16/24/32px), Components/Text's Title story (xl bold, heading level 1), and the new Components/CardRow, Components/ListRow, Components/DetailRow, and Components/LoadingGate stories against docs/manual-tests.md.
