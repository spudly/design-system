New Slider field (adopted from shuoink/ui, design-system #178): a controlled single-value range input with a field `label` and `error` text, `min` / `max` / `step` range props (defaults 0 / 100 / 1), and a scalar `value` + `onValueChange` contract like the other DS fields. It renders a brand-primary fill over the progress track with a round knob on a 44px touch target, supports drag and keyboard adjustment (arrow keys step, Home/End jump to the bounds) with full slider semantics for assistive technology — on native, VoiceOver/TalkBack adjust gestures also step the value — dims and inerts with `disabled`, and can show tick labels under the track via `ticks` ({ value, label } entries — the first and last sit flush with the track ends). A slider with no visible `label` takes its accessible name from an `accessibilityLabel` prop. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Components/Slider's Ticks story shows the One–Ten labels under a 1–10 track with the knob starting at Five, CustomRange steps by 0.5, WithError shows its error line under the slider, and Disabled cannot be dragged or focused.
