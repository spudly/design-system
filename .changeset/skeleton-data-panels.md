Data panels and the remaining data widgets gain skeleton loading states (#278, tranche 2 of 5). NutritionPanel, RatingsCard, RecipeIngredientsPanel, RecipeInstructionsPanel, RecipeOverviewPanel, SecretRevealPanel, StatRow, and TagList each accept a `loading` prop: while loading, their text runs swap for text skeletons at the same sizes and counts, their actions stay in place as inert skeleton buttons, hairlines and dividers render transparent, and no real content, values, or live controls render; the loading region is marked busy for assistive tech. NutritionPanel's loading view-model state renders the ready layout's skeleton (header, one row per nutrient, disclaimer) instead of its former "Estimating nutrition…" line, and RecipeIngredientsPanel's substitutions fetch renders a skeleton substitutions panel instead of a "Finding substitutions…" line. StatRow and TagList mirror StatBand's treatment: StatRow shows exactly 3 dummy stats while loading, and TagList shows one pill skeleton per tag (exactly 3 dummy pills when it has no tags).
