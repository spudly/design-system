New PublicRecipePage and PublicPageFrame exports on `@spudly/ui/pages` — Eciperay's public (signed-out, SEO) recipe surface as a controlled DS page, plus the shared public chrome. PublicRecipePage renders one of four states from its props: a loading spinner; a "Recipe not found" card ("This recipe may be private or no longer available."); a "Recipe unavailable" card ("This recipe link is invalid or has expired."); or the ready recipe — a CTA bar (signed-out: "Sign up free" / "Clone this recipe" / "Get the mobile app"; signed-in: "Save a copy", reading "Saving…" while a clone is in flight, / "Get the mobile app") above a single scroll of overview (hero image, title, status badge, description, Servings/Prep/Cook stats, tag pills), the servings/scale ingredients section with missing-ingredient substitutions, the numbered instructions, and the engagement block (community rating with stale-rating hints, the viewer's star input when signed in, and the app's comments section in its slot). There are no tabs — all sections stay in the document. PublicPageFrame renders the public header (Eciperay brand, Sign in), the wide centered content column, and the footer ("Recipes shared on Eciperay may be viewed without an account." + Privacy Policy / Terms of Service), with navigation leaving through intent callbacks. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: `Pages/Eciperay/PublicRecipePage` (Default, SignedIn, ClonePending, Minimal, Loading, NotFound, Unavailable, Ingredients) and `Pages/Eciperay/PublicPageFrame` (Default, Content, NarrowViewport) — the signed-out page shows all sections in one scroll with no tab bar, the signed-in bar's Save button disables as "Saving…", both error cards show their copy, and the frame's header/footer frame the column at desktop and phone widths.
