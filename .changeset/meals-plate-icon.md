The Meals icon (`MealsIcon` in `@spudly/ui/icons`) is now a divided plate instead of a coffee cup: two quarter portions across the top and one half portion along the bottom, with no rim, drawn in the same stroke style as the module's other icons. Check it anywhere the Meals icon appears (for example the icons gallery in Storybook): the glyph shows the divided plate at every size, and no coffee cup remains. The component name and props are unchanged, so consuming apps need no code changes.
