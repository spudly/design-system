SectionHeading's heading semantics are now explicit and pinned: the shared `SectionHeading` component (the section label over a detail page's Ingredients, Instructions, Cook History, and Nutrition sections) now states the heading role at level 2 in its own source — `accessibilityRole="header"` with `aria-level={2}` — instead of relying on the `subheading` Text variant's default to supply those semantics implicitly. The rendered result is unchanged: `role="heading"` with `aria-level="2"` on web and the `header` accessibility role on native, under the page title's level 1, with visuals unchanged — but the level-2 contract is now guaranteed by SectionHeading itself and covered by its own tests, so a future change to Text's variant defaults cannot silently drop a detail page's section headings from screen-reader heading navigation. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook (Components/SectionHeading): each label is exposed as a level-2 heading in the accessibility panel / rendered DOM, and no `accessibilityrole` attribute appears in the DOM.
