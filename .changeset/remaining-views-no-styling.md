The remaining views no longer apply styling directly (#698 tranche 4): AccessTokensPanel, AccountDataView, CatalogFiltersBar, CatalogHeader, ChunkErrorBoundary, FeedbackPreferenceView, InstallBannerView, LabeledListSection, LazySlot, NutritionGoalsForm, SignInForm, and SubscriptionView now compose reusable widgets for all layout and styling, completing the #698 program — every view in src/views satisfies the no-direct-styling rule (the story-only storyFrame helper aside). The access tokens panel's created-secret warning renders through the new CreatedSecretPanel; the catalog header's stack through the new ListHeaderStack; the lazy slot's skeleton fallback through the new SkeletonPanel; and the new CenteredRow, StartStack, and SpacedTopStack layout widgets carry the centered skeleton row, the start-aligned action stack, and the save action's top separation. Five components that own their styling — AppTopBar, CandidateList, ChangedListBanner, LegalSection, and RatingForm — move from src/views to src/widgets with their stories and tests, so their Storybook entries move from the Views/<Name> group to Widgets/<Name> (Widgets/AppTopBar, Widgets/CandidateList, Widgets/ChangedListBanner, Widgets/LegalSection, Widgets/RatingForm). Names, props, and rendering are unchanged apart from the created-secret panel and the subscription and feedback action rows taking the shared stack treatments.
