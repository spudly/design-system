The remaining views no longer apply styling directly (#698 tranche 4): AccessTokensPanel, AccountDataView, CatalogFiltersBar, CatalogHeader, ChunkErrorBoundary, FeedbackPreferenceView, InstallBannerView, LabeledListSection, LazySlot, NutritionGoalsForm, SignInForm, and SubscriptionView now compose reusable widgets for all layout and styling, completing the #698 program — every view in src/views satisfies the no-direct-styling rule (the story-only storyFrame helper aside). The access tokens panel's created-secret warning renders through the new CreatedSecretPanel; the catalog header's stack through the new ListHeaderStack; the lazy slot's skeleton fallback through the new SkeletonPanel; and the new CenteredRow, StartStack, and SpacedTopStack layout widgets carry the centered skeleton row, the start-aligned action stack, and the save action's top separation. Five components that own their styling — AppTopBar, CandidateList, ChangedListBanner, LegalSection, and RatingForm — move from src/views to src/widgets with their stories and tests, so their Storybook entries move from the Views/<Name> group to Widgets/<Name> (Widgets/AppTopBar, Widgets/CandidateList, Widgets/ChangedListBanner, Widgets/LegalSection, Widgets/RatingForm). Names and props are unchanged, and what a user can observe is unchanged too: every named view and component renders the same headings, fields, panels, and actions in the same order at the same sizes — the app shell's top bar, the profile page's legal section, a recipe's rating form and activation list, and a form's changed-list banner look and behave exactly as before. The one testable difference is in Storybook: the five moved components' stories now render under Widgets/<Name> instead of Views/<Name>.
Stack rebase note (2026-11-20): CatalogFiltersBar's de-styling was rebased over #699/#700, so its searchHelp trigger and searchHint line are preserved — the hint now renders through the Text widget carrying the polite-live-region props instead of a tamagui stack, keeping the view styling-free.
