Two new shared components (#119 extraction set), shipped standalone so every app composes the same units — these are the components PR #308's adoption round inventoried as extraction-required, designed in that tranche's original scope and composed in-file until now: `EqualButtonRow` (a row of action buttons that share the row's width equally: every button grows to an equal share of its line with a 40% minimum width, so two buttons render as equal halves, three render as two halves over one full-width button, and no button shrinks below the floor or wraps its own label; the row owns the 12px gap and the wrapping, and sizes its buttons itself — the caller passes only the buttons. ButtonRow remains the sibling treatment for bars whose buttons keep their natural widths) and `MessageCard` (a whole-screen message state: a surface-filled shell that fills its parent's height and centers a card carrying a heading and one muted explanatory line, both centered, inset 16px from the shell's edges; the caller supplies only the two strings). On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Components/EqualButtonRow (Default: two buttons as equal halves at one height; ThreeButtons: two halves over one full-width button, no wrapped labels; InCard: the same treatment inset by the card's padding) and Components/MessageCard (Default: the card at the frame's vertical center with the heading over the centered muted line; LongMessage: the wrapped message lines stay centered and the taller card stays centered), against docs/manual-tests.md. PublicRecipePage adopts both in its follow-up round: its CTA bar becomes a Card composing EqualButtonRow, and its notFound/unavailable states become MessageCard.
