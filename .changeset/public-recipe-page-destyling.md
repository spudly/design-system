PublicRecipePage is de-styled (#119): the page now composes design-system components only, and its four sections are shared components the recipe surfaces reuse — `RecipeOverviewPanel` (hero image, title, status badge, stat band, tag row), `RecipeIngredientsPanel` (servings stepper, scale selector with presets and a custom value, ingredient rows, substitutions), `RecipeInstructionsPanel` (step cards with the active step highlighted), and `RatingsCard` (community average, stale-rating hints, and the viewer's star input) — alongside three general components: `ScrollShell` (a surface-painted page shell whose single scroll region insets its blocks in a standard column), `EqualButtonRow` (a row whose buttons share the width equally at a 40% minimum, wrapping instead of shrinking), and `MessageCard` (a full-screen centered message card). The page's contract is unchanged: the same props union (loading / notFound / unavailable / ready), the same CTA variants and labels, the same section order in a single scroll, and the same engagement behavior. Two presentation normalizations ride along: the Ingredients and Instructions headings now render in Text's subheading style (xl semibold) and sit 16px from the sections on both sides (previously 24px above the heading and 16px below it), where the shell column's uniform rhythm replaces the hand-mixed padding and margins. On a @spudly/ui bump, re-check in Storybook: Pages/Eciperay/PublicRecipePage's Default, SignedIn, ClonePending, and Minimal stories render the CTA bar, overview, ingredients, and instructions in order with the ratings card and comments slot at the end; its Loading story shows the centered loading gate and its NotFound/Unavailable stories the centered message cards; Components/RecipeIngredientsPanel's Default story steps servings, switches scale presets, and turns the Custom button into a number input; Components/RecipeInstructionsPanel's Default story highlights the tapped step; Components/RatingsCard's Default story shows the stale-rating hints and clears the viewer's rating when the current star is pressed again; Components/EqualButtonRow's Wrapping story wraps its third button at the same width.
